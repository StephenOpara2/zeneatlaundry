Our Services

Professional laundry care tailored to your needs, with pickup and delivery available across Lagos.

🫧

Wash & Fold

Our most popular service. We collect your laundry, wash with premium detergents, tumble dry, and fold everything neatly.

  • Sorted by colour & fabric type
  • Premium, gentle detergents used
  • Neatly folded & bagged
  • 24-hour turnaround available
  • Pickup & delivery included
👔

Dry Cleaning

Expert care for your most delicate and valuable garments — suits, gowns, lace, and more. We treat each item individually.

  • Safe for all delicate fabrics
  • Professional spot treatment
  • Suits, gowns, traditional wear
  • Carefully steamed & pressed
  • Packaged in protective covers

Ironing Only

Already washed your clothes but need that sharp, crisp finish? Schedule a pickup — we'll handle the pressing.

  • Commercial-grade steam irons
  • Crisp, long-lasting press
  • Shirts, trousers, uniforms
  • Same-day option available
  • Hung or folded on request
